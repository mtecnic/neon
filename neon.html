HOW TO RUN THIS CITY
THE GOAL
You're a hardware runner. Buy GPUs, RAM and CPUs where they're cheap, haul them where they're expensive, and climb from STREET RAT to GHOST IN THE GRID. The story of the vanished Broker unfolds as you rise — follow THE LEDGER in the RIGS tab.
THE TRADE LOOP
· QUANTUM DOCKS sells cheap (×0.74) — CORPORATE SPIRE pays rich (×1.24)
· Walk onto a glowing market plaza and press E to trade
· Prices move with night, rain, world events — and your own trades
· SHADOW CIRCUITS pays best at night, but it will rob you
MONEY BEYOND TRADING
· RIGS tab: build inference machines from your hardware (story-critical)
· Bounties refresh at dawn · courier runs from any vendor (F)
· The back-room den deals hi-lo · a drone can trade while you walk
STAYING ALIVE
· The STATUS meter is street heat — cargo + night + rain feed it
· When it trips, crews take cargo or credits · GHOST/IRON RIG cyberware helps
· ENERGY drains over days — below 25% you're FRAYED (slow, visible). Eat noodles, drink at the club, or sleep it off
A PLACE TO LIVE
· The HAB SPIRE door at the park rents rooms — rent charges at dawn
· Sleep to skip to dawn/dusk, wake RESTED (better sells, cooler heat)
· Warehouses store stock off your rig · the club sells useful buffs
